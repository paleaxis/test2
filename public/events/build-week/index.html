EVENT.01 — upcoming

Build Week — First Hackathon

48 hours, one real problem, real tools. The goal is shipped, not slides.

Date November 20, 2026
Location DCITC Lab & online
Duration 48 hours
Level All levels

01 — About

What this is.

Event brief

Our first 48-hour build event. Teams pick a problem from a provided list (or their own), use the club's stack guides, and must deliver something runnable by Sunday noon. Mentors from all study groups float between teams. Judging is 40% working software, 40% engineering judgment, 20% documentation.
