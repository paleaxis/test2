02 — Expanded record

The summaries, expanded.

2024 · MIL.01 +

The club rebuilds itself as a study organization rather than an events-only club.

  • Study groups founded — Three reading groups launched: Systems, Algorithms, and AI/ML. Each meets weekly and produces public notes.
  • First Linux workshop — A sold-out terminal workshop for 40 students — the template every workshop since has followed.
  • VoltLab begins — A physics department request starts the electronics track; the first prototype runs in the club lab.
2025 · MIL.02 +

The first shipped software, a department adoption, and a national CTF finish.

  • Three projects shipped — PadChat enters the computer lab, VoltLab is adopted by the physics department, and CampusMesh covers two lecture halls.
  • National CTF top 20 — A mixed team of four reaches the top 20 in a national capture-the-flag — the club's first competitive placement.
  • Journal launches — The Tech Journal publishes its first issue: a systems post and a field report from VoltLab.
2026 · MIL.03 +

Open source, 120+ members, and infrastructure the club runs itself.

  • Nodhini goes open source — The Bengali NLP toolkit releases under MIT; two external contributors submit PRs within a month.
  • 100+ active members — Membership passes 120, spread across six study groups and four project teams.
  • Build Week — The first 48-hour hackathon ships nine runnable projects and becomes a standing event.
  • Self-hosted infra — The club moves its journal, mirrors, and CTF infrastructure onto its own hardware.
