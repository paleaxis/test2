Curate. Build. Understand.

00 — Join

CURIOSITY IS THE ONLY PREREQUISITE.

You do not need to be the best programmer in the room. You need to be the one who reads the manual, asks the question, and shows up twice.

Apply by email → Ask us first

01 — Why join

What membership actually gives you.

A / LEARN

Fundamentals, in order

A structured path from terminal to kernel, from loops to system calls — via study groups with public notes.

B / BUILD

Real projects

Your name on a shipped, documented system. The club's repos are the resume.

C / TEACH

A room that reads

Mentors, reviewers, and a culture where 'let me check the manual' is a compliment.

02 — The path

Your first semester, roughly.

01

Pick a study group

Systems, algorithms, AI/ML, web, security, or electronics. Sit in on one session first — you are welcome as a guest.

02

Finish the onboarding track

A self-paced track: terminal, Git, a first repo. Roughly four hours, entirely doable, entirely worth it.

03

Make one contribution

A typo fix counts. Then a real issue. Then a feature. Review is kind, specific, and available.

04

Ship something small

By the end of the semester, a project you can show someone — however small, it has to work.

03 — Who it's for

This club is for you if…

01 You have asked "but how does that actually work?" in the last month.
02 You would rather understand a small thing deeply than skim ten big things.
03 You are comfortable not knowing, as long as you get to find out.
04 You want to build things that are used by real people — classmates, teachers, the lab.

04 — How to join

One email, one meeting.

Send a short email introducing yourself — name, year, and one thing you want to understand this semester. We reply within a week and invite you to a study group as a guest. No forms, no interviews, no fees for the first semester.

Write to itclub@dhakacollege.edu.bd →
