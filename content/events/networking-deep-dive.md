## About the Event

Nexus is a gathering for people who like taking things apart just to understand how they work.

Across three days, participants will explore emerging technologies, practical engineering, digital culture, and experimental projects through talks, workshops, competitions, and open discussions.

Whether you're writing your first program or building something nobody has tried before, there's something to poke at.

## Featured Sessions

### The Machine Beneath the Machine

A practical look at what actually happens between a line of code and the hardware executing it. Processes, memory, system calls, scheduling, and a few things that probably should have been documented.

**01:30 PM · Main Hall**

---

### Designing for Humans

Technology does not become useful simply because it works. This session explores interfaces, visual hierarchy, accessibility, and the small decisions that make software understandable.

**03:00 PM · Studio 02**

### Breaking the Surface

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can attend?

Anyone interested in technology, software, design, research, or digital culture can attend. No particular academic background is required.

### Do I need programming experience?

No. Several sessions are designed for beginners, while technical workshops provide deeper material for experienced participants.

### Is registration required?

Yes. Some workshops have limited capacity, so registration is recommended before the event.

### Can I present my project?

Yes. Project submissions are open until **31 October 2026**. Selected projects will be displayed during the final showcase.

### What should I bring?

Bring your laptop if you're attending a workshop. Everything else is optional, including the inevitable collection of cables you haven't used since 2022.
